CLA96G Part / Unit architecture and objective crosswalk

This page is the coverage contract for the self-paced course. The IBM course guides define the Part and Unit structure; Skunkworks Academy adds theory, simulations, scenarios, retrieval practice and assessment under those boundaries rather than substituting a different curriculum structure.

The objective IDs below are internal Skunkworks identifiers used to connect course content, mastery, the assessment bank and incident scenarios. They are not IBM objective codes.

Coverage baseline76

IBM unit objectives represented in the course model.

Official structure4 parts · 16 units

Skunkworks enrichment is nested beneath the source structure rather than replacing it.

Evidence modelLearn → practise → assess

Every unit feeds knowledge checks, practical evidence, assessment sampling or incident practice.

# DBA Incident Centre

**Recommended time:** 5 hours of deliberate practice · **12 incidents across Parts 1–4**

The Incident Centre converts CLA96G concepts into production-style decisions. You are not rewarded for making the fastest change or clicking every diagnostic option. You are rewarded for selecting **discriminating evidence**, forming a causal hypothesis, protecting recovery/security controls and choosing the smallest justified intervention.

<CourseProgress compact />

## Incident-response operating model

Use the same discipline on every scenario:

1. **Stabilize the situation** — understand business impact and avoid destructive actions.
2. **Prioritize evidence** — you have a limited evidence budget; choose signals that separate competing hypotheses.
3. **Form a causal hypothesis** — state what mechanism best explains the symptom.
4. **Choose the smallest justified action** — control blast radius and preserve rollback.
5. **Validate** — compare technical and user-facing outcomes with the baseline.
6. **Document** — preserve commands, evidence, decisions and follow-up actions.

:::tip How scoring works
Each incident is worth 100 points. Up to 50 points come from collecting **relevant** evidence and 50 from the operational decision. Irrelevant evidence consumes the investigation budget and reduces the evidence score. An incident is recorded as solved only when the decision is sound and the total score is at least **75%**.
:::

## Coverage

The 12 scenarios deliberately span the four Parts:

- **Part 1:** wrong-instance change, instance/listener startup failure, restore/rollforward-pending recovery.
- **Part 2:** LOAD/table state, archive-log pressure, HADR replay lag.
- **Part 3:** lock chain, deadlock after transaction change, least-privilege/RCAC authorization path.
- **Part 4:** stale-statistics plan regression, index/workload regression, temporary-space/sort capacity pressure.

Each incident displays the internal objective IDs it exercises so the scenario can be traced back to the course crosswalk.

## Post-incident reflection

After each solved incident, record:

- Which signal most strongly changed your hypothesis?
- Which evidence option was tempting but low-value?
- Which intervention would have increased blast radius?
- What alert or preventive control could detect the condition earlier?
- What evidence belongs in the permanent incident record?

## Build your own incident runbook

```text
Incident ID / date / business impact
Current Db2 code level and database role
Symptom timeline
Evidence budget / evidence selected
Root-cause hypothesis
Change / intervention and approval
Rollback position
Validation evidence
Follow-up prevention action
```

## Rules

- 24 questions.
- Answer every question before submission.
- **80% or higher** meets the course mastery threshold.
- After submission you will see rationales and domain scores.
- If you score below 80%, revisit domains below threshold, repeat the relevant lab and retake.
- The browser stores your best percentage locally; it is not an accredited certification record.

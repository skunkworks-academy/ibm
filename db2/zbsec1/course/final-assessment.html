After passing​

Retain a learning evidence bundle containing:

  1. capstone architecture;
  2. access/SoD matrix;
  3. DDF security review;
  4. row/column policy test matrix;
  5. cryptographic/DR dependency map;
  6. audit incident timeline;
  7. compliance control-to-evidence matrix;
  8. one incident playbook;
  9. final assessment score;
  10. a personal remediation/next-learning plan.

For workplace use, replace synthetic evidence with organisation-approved, redacted evidence and obtain review by the relevant security/database/risk owners.

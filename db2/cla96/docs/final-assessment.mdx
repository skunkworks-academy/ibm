# Final assessment: objective-balanced production-readiness scenarios

**Recommended time:** 2 hours including review

This assessment measures integrated DBA judgment across the four official CLA96G Parts. It deliberately favors **scenario reasoning, operational evidence and controlled decision-making** over syntax recall.

<CourseProgress compact />

## Assessment rules

- The assessment unlocks only after the Part 1–4 mastery gates are satisfied.
- The bank contains **64 original questions**, four for each of the 16 official CLA96G units.
- Each attempt contains **24 randomized questions**: six from each Part.
- The selection guarantees broad official-unit coverage; answer order is randomized independently.
- Overall pass threshold: **75%**.
- Minimum Part floor: **60% in every Part**. A high overall score cannot hide a major domain weakness.
- After submission, every item shows a rationale plus its internal objective/unit coverage ID.
- The result view shows Part scores and links directly to the weakest unit(s) for targeted remediation.
- A passing attempt records the final-assessment milestone and reports score/pass status to a compatible SCORM 1.2 LMS when its API is available.
- The exported completion record is browser-local evidence for this independent learning companion; it is not an IBM certification or official IBM course completion record.

:::tip Assessment strategy
For each scenario, choose the response that best preserves evidence, limits blast radius, respects recovery/security controls and tests the strongest causal hypothesis. Several distractors are intentionally plausible DBA actions taken at the wrong time or without enough evidence.
:::
